01

A Son of the Rift

Born in Eldoret and forged in the memories of Kapchorua and Kericho. My identity is built on a foundation of Love, Justice, and Honesty, tempered by a past that tested the very limits of my soul.

"I carry the triumph of that 60km journey toward home—a journey that led me not just to a destination, but to the grace of a father who taught me the power of letting go."
02

The Ascent

I am a survivor of a system that tried to cage my spirit. I carry the scars of isolation, betrayal, and the heavy weight of a youth spent fighting for my freedom.

Today, I am a Pioneer of African Excellence. I have turned the energy once spent on survival into a relentless pursuit of creation—building dozens of platforms, websites, and mobile worlds.

03

My heart beats for the history of my people, from the Kenyatta Archives to the Nairobi Museum. Through every app and website I build, I am proving that excellence is our birthright and that our brightest days are crafted by our own hands.
