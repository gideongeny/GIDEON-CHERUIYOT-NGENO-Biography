01

A Son of the Rift

Born in Eldoret and forged in the memories of Kapchorua and Kericho. My identity is built on a foundation of Love, Justice, and Honesty, tempered by a past that tested the very limits of my soul. I carry the triumph of that 60km journey toward home—a journey that led me not just to a physical destination, but to the grace of a father who taught me the power of letting go.

02

The Ascent from Captivity

I am a survivor of a system that tried to cage my spirit. I carry the scars of isolation, betrayal, and the heavy weight of a youth spent fighting for my freedom. But the cage was not strong enough. I turned the energy once spent on survival into a relentless pursuit of creation.

Today, I am a Pioneer. I build not just websites, but digital worlds that preserve our heritage and project our future excellence.

03

The Digital Empire

From the Kenyatta Archives to the Nairobi Museum, my heart beats for the history of my people. I have built over 85 platforms, each a testament to the fact that our brightest days are crafted by our own hands. This is the "Architect's Manifesto"—where code meets compassion.
